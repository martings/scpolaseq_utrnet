# Scientific model and limits

For population `c`, PAS `p` and gene `g`, observed usage is pooled Sierra UMI count at `p` divided by total pooled gene PAS UMIs in curated cells of `c`. This is a descriptive estimator; zero total UMI produces missing usage. The model does not assume exactly two PAS.

For an isoform model `i` associated with `p` and regulatory region `r`, the baseline candidate score is:

`usage(c,p) × model_weight(i|p) × site_present(i,r) × accessibility(i,r) × evidence_factor(r) × abundance_factor(c,regulator)`.

Each factor is exported separately. `model_weight` splits a PAS among distinct compatible sequence models and marks ambiguity rather than asserting one transcript. Evidence factors are ordered heuristics (motif 0.25, computational site 0.35, external database 0.55, experimental other context 0.75, matching context 1.0). They are model choices, not calibrated probabilities. Measured regulator abundance is the mean mRNA UMI per curated cell, normalized across populations with a small pseudocount. Missing accessibility or abundance is neutrally imputed to 1 only for a **partial** score; `score_status` identifies it. A predicted edge score is never molecular occupancy. Jeffreys Dirichlet marginal intervals propagate UMI sampling uncertainty to scores and heuristic pairwise difference bounds; these intervals do not capture cell dependence or biological variation and are not joint-confidence intervals.

Transcript models come from GTF exon/CDS/UTR features. PAS must fall in the terminal exon and in a 3′UTR interval downstream of the CDS in transcript orientation. Candidate sequence is extracted through the PAS from the indexed FASTA, reverse complemented on the minus strand. Alternate terminal exons and splicing models are retained as alternatives. A site outside compatible annotated 3′UTRs has no sequence-derived motif edge. The gene-wide PAS coordinate span is exported and marked suspicious when it exceeds the configured limit.

AUUUA is a generic ARE pentamer. The bundled ELAVL1/ZFP36 motif associations are supported by ATtRACT experimental records from other contexts; a match in SAT1 remains a computational prediction, not PBMC binding evidence. Specific occupancy requires direct experimental evidence and cell-context assessment. MirGeneDB-derived exact 7mer-m8 miRNA seed matches and imported miRNA sites are prior-only until independent small-RNA abundance or binding evidence is supplied. RNAplfold accessibility is optional, parameterized, and cached by sequence and parameters; no folding result is fabricated when the executable is absent.

Graph structural edges (gene/PAS/isoform/region) are distinct from candidate binding and candidate regulation edges. `site_retention.csv` states whether a genomic site is present in every compatible candidate model, restricted to some PAS, or dependent on an ambiguous transcript model; unresolved PAS are listed separately. Rewiring compares population scores by regulator and genomic region and labels gained/lost availability based on nonzero observed isoform usage. Graphs are exported for all curated cell types, including small populations; `population_coverage_status` flags those below the default 15-cell minimum for cautious interpretation. No independent biological replicates exist in PBMC 1k, so all cross-lineage findings are exploratory.

`population_metrics.csv` groups repeated isoform rows by regulator and genomic region before computing score concentration. `sensitivity.csv` likewise compares region-level candidate rankings under graph-only model perturbations. Its top-ten overlap can remain unchanged when related miRNAs share seed sites, so the full scenario scores and evidence classes should be inspected. These checks test the scoring model; they do not estimate biological variability.
